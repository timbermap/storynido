# Frequently asked questions

## How it works

### What will my child look like in the book?

From 1 to 3 photos (or by choosing their features, with no photos) we create an illustrated character inspired by your child, who appears on every page in the style you choose. First we show you their character sheet so you can confirm the likeness; if something is off, you fix it before moving on.

### Can I see the book before paying?

Yes. The preview is free: you see the character, the cover and the first illustrated pages. You only pay if you love it.

### Can I change the text or an illustration?

Yes. In the preview you can edit the text of each page, try another illustration for a specific page and correct the character's features.

### Can I use the same hero in another story?

Yes. We save their details in your account: in your next story you pick them and everything is filled in. If you created them by choosing features, those are reused as they are; if you used photos, as long as they are still stored (they are deleted after 30 days).

### What ages are the stories for?

For children up to 12. Each story shows its recommended ages and the text adapts to the hero's age. For ages 9 to 12 there are longer stories: mystery, science and exploration.

### Which languages are available?

Spanish, English and Portuguese.

## Photos and privacy

### What happens to the photos I upload?

They are stored encrypted, only our systems use them to create your book and they are automatically deleted after 30 days. You can delete them earlier from your account. They are never used to train artificial intelligence models.

### Can I create the book without uploading photos?

Yes. You choose the skin tone, hair, eyes and whether they wear glasses, and we create the character with those features.

### How do I delete my data?

From My account you can delete each child's profile (with their photos and drafts), each story in progress or your whole account.

## Prices, payments and shipping

### How much does it cost?

You can see it on each story and before paying: it depends on the format (digital, softcover or hardcover) and, for printed books, on the shipping country. Prices are in US dollars (USD).

### Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once for each book: there are no subscriptions or recurring charges.

### How long does it take to arrive?

The digital book arrives by email within minutes. The printed book is produced in 2 to 5 business days and then shipped; before paying we show you the estimated time for your country.

### Do you ship to my country?

The digital book is available in almost every country. For printed books, on the shipping page you can choose your country and see whether we print for it and how long it takes.

### Is it a good gift?

Yes! You can mark the order as a gift, add a message and send it straight to another address.

### What if the book arrives with a problem?

If the digital file has an error, we fix it or refund you (let us know within 14 days). If the printed book arrives damaged or defective, send us photos within 14 days of receiving it and we reprint it at no cost or refund you.

## Celebrations

### What does the kit include?

A mini story starring the guest of honor and, if you like, activities, an invitation, a thank-you card, stickers and favor tags. Everything comes from the same design and is downloaded to print at home or at a print shop; the license says how many copies you can make.

### Which occasions is it for?

Birthdays, baptisms, First Communions and graduations or the end of the school year. Each occasion has its own wording in the story and the pieces.

## Professionals

### How does it work for teachers and therapists?

You create a project for each child with an alias (never a surname) and no clinical data, choose the story and its goal, and by default the character is built from chosen features, without photos. It is not a subscription: the more stories you buy in a month, the bigger your discount.
