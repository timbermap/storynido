# Frequently asked questions

### How does my child look in the book?

From 1 to 3 photos we create an illustrated character inspired by your child, who appears on every page in the style you choose. Before paying, you see the cover and two pages for free.

### What happens to the photos I upload?

They are stored encrypted, used only by our systems to create your book, and automatically deleted after 30 days. You can delete them sooner from your account. They are never used to train artificial intelligence models. If you prefer not to upload photos, you can create the character by choosing their features.

### Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once for each book: no subscriptions and no recurring charges.

### How long does it take?

The digital book arrives by email within minutes. The printed book is produced in 2 to 5 business days and then shipped; we show you the estimated time for your country before you pay.

### Can I change the text?

Yes. In the preview you can edit the text, change the photo and regenerate individual pages.

### What ages are the stories for?

For children from 0 to 12. Each story shows the recommended ages, and the text adapts to the main character's age.

### Which languages are available?

Spanish, English and Portuguese.

### Is it a good gift?

Yes! You can mark the order as a gift, add a message and ship it straight to another address.

### What if I don't like the result?

That's why the preview is free. If there's a problem with your book, check our refund policy or contact us.
