# Cookie policy

We use cookies and similar technologies so the site works and, only if you accept, to analyze its use and show you relevant ads.

## Types of cookies

| Type      | Purpose                                                     | Requires your consent? |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Necessary | Sign-in, language, country, security and your cookie choice | No                     |
| Analytics | Understanding how the site is used (Google Analytics 4)     | Yes                    |
| Marketing | Measuring campaigns and showing ads (Meta Pixel)            | Yes                    |

## How to change your choice

You can change your preferences at any time from the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of every page. You can also delete cookies from your browser.
