# Shipping policy

## Where we ship

We ship printed books to the countries listed on the site. We print in your country or region so delivery is faster and has a lower environmental impact.

## Timing

Production: 2 to 5 business days. Shipping: depends on your country, shown before you pay. We will email you the tracking number.

## Costs

Shipping costs are shown at checkout before you pay.

## Digital book

The PDF is sent by email, usually within minutes, with a download link.

## Shipping problems

If your order doesn't arrive within the estimated time or arrives damaged, write to us using the [contact form](/en#contact).
