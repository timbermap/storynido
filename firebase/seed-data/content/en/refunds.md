# Refund policy

We want you to love your book. That's why you can see a **free preview** before paying.

## Digital book

If there is a problem with the file (for example, missing pages or a generation error), we will fix it or refund you. Contact us within 14 days of purchase.

## Printed book

Since every book is unique and personalized, we don't accept returns for change of mind once it has been sent to print. If the book arrives damaged or with a printing defect, send us photos within 14 days of delivery and we will reprint it at no cost or refund you.

## Cancellations

You can cancel an order at no cost as long as it hasn't been sent to print.

## How to request a refund

Email hola@storynido.com with your order number. Refunds go back to the original payment method, within your bank's processing times.
