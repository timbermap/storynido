# Privacy policy

At StoryNido we take special care of children's data. This policy explains what data we process, why, for how long, and your rights. Data controller: StoryNido SRL, Uruguay, hola@storynido.com.

## What data we process

- **Your account:** email, name, language, country and sign-in method.
- **The child:** name, age, pronouns (optional), interests, pet's and best friend's names, dedication, and 1 to 3 photos.
- **Your orders:** shipping address (and, if you add it for deliveries to Mexico, Argentina or Brazil, the recipient's tax ID, which the printer uses only for shipping and customs), products, amounts and status. Payment details are processed by the payment provider; we never see or store your card.
- **Usage:** technical data and, only if you accept, analytics and marketing cookies.
- **Approximate location:** we use your IP address to infer your country and show the site in your language. The lookup runs on our server with the [DB-IP](https://db-ip.com) database (CC BY 4.0 license); we don't store or share your IP.

## Why we use it

To create and deliver the book, manage your account and orders, provide support, prevent fraud and, if you agree, send you news. We do not sell your data or use it to train artificial intelligence models.

## Photos of children

- They are only uploaded with your **explicit consent** as a responsible adult, which we record with the date and text version.
- They are stored encrypted in private storage, and only our automated systems access them.
- They are **automatically deleted after 30 days**, and you can delete them sooner, at any time, from your account.
- We use them only to generate the illustrations for your book.

## Who we share it with

With providers that help us deliver the service, under confidentiality agreements: hosting and cloud (Google Cloud), artificial intelligence (Google Vertex AI or an equivalent provider), payments, printing and transactional email. They only receive what they need for their task.

## How long we keep it

Photos: 30 days at most. Child details and books: while you keep your account or until you ask us to delete them. Billing data: as long as required by law.

## Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or request a copy. You can delete photos and profiles from your account or email hola@storynido.com. We respond within the legal deadlines (Uruguay's Personal Data Protection Law No. 18.331, GDPR, COPPA, Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data and Brazil's LGPD, as applicable).

## Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access, and short-lived download links.

## Changes

We will notify you of any important change to this policy.
