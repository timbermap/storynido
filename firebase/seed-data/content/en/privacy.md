# Privacy policy

At StoryNido we take special care of children's data. This policy explains what data we process, what for, on what basis, for how long and what rights you have. Data controller: StoryNido SRL, Uruguay. Privacy contact: hola@storynido.com.

## What data we process

- **Your account:** email address, name, language, country and sign-in method.
- **The child:** name, age, pronouns (optional), interests, the name of their pet and best friend, dedication, memories you choose to include and 1 to 3 photos.
- **Your orders:** shipping address (and, if you enter it for shipments to Mexico, Argentina or Brazil, the recipient's tax ID, which the print shop uses only for shipping and customs), products, amounts and status. Payment data is processed by the payment provider; we never see or store your card.
- **Usage:** technical data and, only if you accept, analytics and marketing cookies. No analytics or advertising runs in the creator, checkout or orders, except a notice that a purchase happened (amount and currency, with no child data), and only with your consent.
- **Site errors:** if something fails in your browser, we receive the error message and the page (without parameters or identifiers) so we can fix it. It contains no personal data.
- **Approximate location:** we infer your country from your IP address to show the site in your language. The lookup runs on our server with the [DB-IP](https://db-ip.com) database (CC BY 4.0); we don't store or share the IP.

## What we use it for and on what basis

- **Creating and delivering your book, managing your account and orders, giving you support:** necessary to perform our contract with you.
- **The child's photos:** only with your express consent as parent or guardian, which you can withdraw at any time.
- **Preventing fraud and abuse and keeping the service secure:** our legitimate interest.
- **Meeting legal and tax obligations.**
- **Analytics, advertising and email news:** only if you accept.

We don't sell your data or use it to train artificial intelligence models.

## Children

StoryNido is aimed at adults. Accounts belong to parents and guardians; we don't collect data directly from children or show them ads. The child's data is provided by the responsible adult, who can view, correct and delete it from their account at any time. If you believe a child gave us data on their own, write to us and we will delete it.

## Photos of minors

- They are uploaded only with your **express consent** as the responsible adult, which we record with the date and version of the text.
- They are stored encrypted in private storage, and only our automated systems can access them.
- They are **deleted automatically after 30 days**, and you can delete them earlier from your account whenever you like.
- We use them only to generate your book's illustrations.

## Artificial intelligence

To create the text and illustrations we use third-party AI services (today, Google Vertex AI). We send only what is needed to generate your book (for example, the photo or traits of the character and the chosen story), under business terms that don't allow that data to be used to train their models. Results go through automated quality and content checks.

## Who we share it with

With providers that help us deliver the service, under confidentiality agreements: hosting and cloud (Google Cloud), artificial intelligence (Google Vertex AI or an equivalent provider), payments, print shops and transactional email. They only receive what their task requires. We may also share data if a competent authority requires it by law.

## International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. When that happens we require adequate safeguards (for example, contractual clauses) and limit what is sent.

## How long we keep it

Photos: 30 days at most. The child's data and books: while you have your account or until you ask us to delete them. If you delete your account we remove your personal data and your children's data; we keep paid orders (amounts and billing data) for as long as the law requires. Technical security logs: short periods.

## Your rights

You can access, correct and delete your data, object to its use, ask for a copy, restrict processing and withdraw your consent at any time (without affecting what was already done). You can delete photos, profiles and your account from your account, or write to hola@storynido.com. We reply within the legal deadlines (Uruguay's Law 18,331 on Personal Data Protection, GDPR, COPPA, Mexico's Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties and Brazil's LGPD, as applicable). If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country; in Uruguay, the Unidad Reguladora y de Control de Datos Personales (URCDP).

## Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access and download links that expire quickly. If an incident affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

## Changes

We will tell you about any important change to this policy, by email or on the site.
