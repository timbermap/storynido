Choosing a birthday gift for a child can be tricky: many toys are forgotten within a week. If you're looking for something **memorable**, here are a few meaningful ideas.

## 1. A book where they are the hero

A personalized story with their name, face and favorite things is a gift they open with surprise and reread for years. It only takes a few minutes to create and can be shipped straight to their door.

## 2. A shared experience

A museum visit, a picnic or an afternoon cooking together. Kids remember moments more than things.

## 3. Something to create

Paints, building blocks or a craft kit invite them to imagine and play without screens.

## 4. A memory box

A decorated box to keep drawings, tickets and photos from the year. Over time, it becomes a treasure.

## Tips to get it right

- **Think about what they love today**, not a year ago: at this age interests change fast.
- **Add a handwritten note.** A few personal lines make any gift unique.
- **Plan ahead** if the gift is printed or shipped, so it arrives before the party.

A great gift doesn't have to be big: it just has to say "I thought of you."
